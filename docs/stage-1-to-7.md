# The stages explained

Seven stages. Early testers said they felt like one run with a different
backdrop, which was fair at the time. Each one now asks for something the others
do not, and this page covers what each one is and how to pass it.

Everything is generated from the day's market. The clock and the pass conditions
below are fixed. The level, the people in it and how crowded it gets change every
morning.

**Attacker counts are not fixed.** Each stage has a crowding setting, and the
actual number comes from the day's chart and the Fear and Greed index. A fearful,
volatile day puts more in the sky than a calm one. The numbers in brackets below
are from one real day, to show the shape.

| | Stage | World | Clock | Crowding | To pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily Humiliation Ritual | Chart | 1:50 | 0.7 | Reach extraction, take the relic |
| 2 | Shutdown Tour | Chart | 1:50 | 0.9 | Reach extraction, 4 caches, 2 out |
| 3 | Exploit Afterparty | Chart | 1:45 | 1.25 | Reach extraction, 12 attackers, above 50% hull |
| 4 | Clarity Circus | Chart, watched | 1:40 | 1.4 | Reach extraction, the relic, 6 caches |
| 5 | Institutional Face Escort | City | 1:40 | 1.55 | Reach extraction, 4 out |
| 6 | Narrative Thunderdome | City | 2:30 | 1.8 | Reach extraction, 3 out |
| 7 | Save Face | Ring city | 4:10 | 0.35 | Reach extraction, 5 out, the relic, 8 caches |

The game shows you these conditions on the results screen when you miss one, with
what you actually managed next to it.

---

## 1. Daily Humiliation Ritual

**To pass:** reach extraction and take the relic.

The introduction. Today's worst performer in the top 100 becomes the terrain: its
real 24-hour chart, pulled at midnight UTC, is the ground you fly over. A sharp
drop is a wall you have to climb. A slow bleed is a long descent with nowhere to
stand.

Five people are trapped in it. They are the accounts crypto X spent the day
discussing, with their real handles and pictures, and every line the game shows
links back to the post it came from.

The emptiest sky in the game (about 10 attackers on a normal day). Nothing is
hidden and nothing is watching you. Fly, free people, reach the pad.

## 2. Shutdown Tour

**To pass:** reach extraction with 4 caches and 2 people.

Same sky, more to do in it. The stage turns from "can you fly this" into "can you
carry things while flying this". Somebody on your back changes how the ship
handles, and one of the five is heavy enough that carrying him costs you real
speed.

Crowding rises to 0.9 (about 14 attackers). The clock is unchanged.

## 3. Exploit Afterparty

**To pass:** reach extraction, clear 12 attackers, and finish above 50% hull.

The first stage you cannot avoid your way through. It is also the only stage that
asks for a hull number, so getting the kills is not enough on its own. You have to
get them without being taken apart.

Crowding 1.25 (about 25 attackers) and five seconds less on the clock. The first
stage where your choice of gun matters.

## 4. Clarity Circus

**To pass:** reach extraction with the relic and 6 caches.

The last chart stage, and the one that changes what an attacker is. From here they
have **sight cones**. Cross one and the level wakes: they stop patrolling and walk
toward the noise. The ones that have not seen you can be walked around entirely.

That turns a fight into a choice about whether to have one. Crowding 1.4 (about 31
attackers), and the fastest way through is to avoid most of them.

## 5. Institutional Face Escort

**To pass:** reach extraction with 4 people.

The chart is gone. The same price data drawn as **bars** instead of a line already
looks like a skyline, so the streets between the buildings are the gaps between
the bars. Same seed, same numbers, drawn differently. A volatile day gives a
jagged city full of cover. A flat day gives long open avenues with nowhere to
hide.

There is a car. It is twice as fast and heard from twice as far. A doorway fits a
person but not the car, so going inside means getting out. Driving through
somebody at speed hurts them, and twice puts them down.

Crowding 1.55 (about 37 attackers).

## 6. Narrative Thunderdome

**To pass:** reach extraction with 3 people.

The only stage where the verb is reading. Panels around the district each show
**four posts that went out today**. One of them explains why the day happened.
Pick it and the panel opens. Pick wrong and everyone within a street starts
walking toward the noise.

The exit stays shut until every panel is read, so in practice you have to read
them all before the pass condition is even reachable.

Two and a half minutes, the longest clock before the finale, and deliberately so.
Ten panels of four posts each at two minutes worked out to twelve seconds a panel
including travel, which is skimming rather than reading. A timer that punishes
reading turns this stage into guessing.

Crowding 1.8 (about 41 attackers), the busiest in the game.

## 7. Save Face

**To pass:** reach extraction with 5 people, the relic and 8 caches.

A third kind of world, and the only stage that is not mainly about shooting.

**Concentric rings around a core.** You start outside and work inward. There is no
forward, only closer and further out, and the only way through a wall is the one
gap in it. The gaps are offset so leaving one never points at the next, which is
what makes this circling a wall rather than running a corridor.

**No weapon opens a gate.** Each gap is guarded by a question about the projects
that were here before this cycle and will be here after it. Reaching one of them
gives you its intel: where it ranks by size, and how it is holding up while today
falls apart. The gate then asks about projects behind it and shows only their
tickers, so you can only answer if you went and looked. You can reach a gate
without having done that. You just cannot answer it.

The projects are the largest by market capitalisation on the day you play, from
the same market call that picks the day's wreck. Nobody curates the list.
Stablecoins are filtered out because a peg is designed not to move, so it has not
survived anything.

Crowding drops to 0.35 (about 12 attackers), the emptiest sky after stage one. The
finale is decided by working things out, and a crowded arena would drown that
rather than make it harder.

Four minutes and ten seconds, the longest run in the game.

## What carries between stages

**Face** is the score and it adds up. It opens later stages, weapons and a
steadier gun.

**The relic** is a single object on most stages, and three pass conditions depend
on it. It is always worth the detour.

**Caches** pay in the day's own token, which is the money inside a run. It dies
with the run and the ticker changes tomorrow, so nobody builds up an advantage and
none of it is for sale.

**Aim assist** starts at a baseline for everyone and rises with progress. A staked
challenge pins both sides to the baseline, because two people betting on one seed
have to be playing the same game.
