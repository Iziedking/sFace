Every day the worst performer in the top 100 becomes the level. Its real
24-hour chart is the ground you fly over, Fear and Greed decides how crowded the
sky is, and the people trapped in the wreck are whoever crypto X spent
that day arguing about. Nobody draws the level. The market does, at midnight UTC.

Seven stages, each asking something the others do not. Fly the chart. Do not be
seen. Drive a city built from the same price data drawn as bars. Read four posts
that genuinely went out today and pick the one that explains it. Then work
inward through a ring city where no weapon opens a gate, and the answer is the
only key.

It is built for people who already follow the market. If you know how the majors
moved today, you can answer a gate on sight.

Nimiq Pay is the identity and the money. Your wallet signs each score over the
date, seed, stage and number. The service verifies the Ed25519 signature and
derives the address from the public key, so a row belongs to a wallet rather
than to a device id anyone can regenerate. Every row publishes its signature,
so anyone can check it without trusting us. Challenges are staked in NIM and
settle wallet to wallet. sFace never holds funds.

Scores are bounded by rebuilding the level from its seed and refusing what that
level cannot pay. Nothing that helps you is for sale.
