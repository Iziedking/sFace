# Sface living-world art direction

## Character construction

- Adult editorial proportions: 5 to 5.5 heads tall in key art, 4.5 heads tall
  in gameplay. Avoid childlike oversized heads and fashion-illustration anatomy.
- Faces use grounded asymmetry, visible age, and restrained expressions.
- Hands, tools, and footwear must explain the character's work at a glance.
- Explorer silhouette: compact, upright, orange field jacket, scanner, notebook.
- Builder silhouette: broad, relaxed, seafoam overshirt, tester, repair roll.
- Mara silhouette: stable triangular apron, orange scarf, keys, tablet, lantern.
- Clothing is practical and repaired. No armor, cape, token logo, or uniformed
  superhero language.

## Sface palette

- Canvas cream: `#f4ede0`
- Paper cream: `#eadfc8`
- Ink: `#14110e`
- Nimiq orange: `#ff5a1f`
- Orange pale: `#ffd9c4`
- Seafoam: `#8fb3a8`
- Harbor water: `#8fc8c2`
- Workwear charcoal: `#292725`
- Leather: `#65513b`

## Shape and line rules

- Use 7 to 8 pixel outer contours at the 1200 pixel source width.
- Use rounded joins on bodies and tools; architecture may use firm corners.
- Use flat fills plus one shadow value. Avoid airbrushed gradients and noisy
  surface detail.
- Every character must remain identifiable as a black silhouette at 96 pixels.
- Important gameplay objects use orange plus shape or icon differences; color
  alone is never authoritative.

## World composition

- Foreground frames the scene without covering the walkable route.
- Cream stone paths remain the highest-value navigable surface.
- Orange identifies human intent, lanterns, and active infrastructure.
- Seafoam identifies water, transport, and restored network flow.
- Each district needs one human reason, one visible failure, and one visible
  restoration result.

## Production gate

The SVG sources are editable construction references. Do not export sprite
atlases until the owner approves this direction and a small-size silhouette test
passes at 320, 390, and 430 CSS pixels.
